One joke about Monday mornings, at most 200 characters, written by a tiny
self-contained Node 22 program. `main.ts` embeds the joke as a literal and
writes it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`.

> My bed filed a complaint against Monday morning. The alarm sided with
> Monday. My coffee is still in arbitration.
